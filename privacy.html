Privacy Policy

Flex Video Uploader · Flex Tech Solutions · Last updated 17 November 2026

Flex Video Uploader (the "App") is a Windows desktop application. This policy explains what data it uses and where that data goes.

1. Summary

The App runs entirely on your own computer. We do not operate any server, and we do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

2. Data the App uses

  • Videos, titles and descriptions that you choose from your computer.
  • Access tokens that TikTok and Facebook give the App when you log in, so it can post on your behalf.
  • Basic TikTok account information (such as display name and allowed privacy options) to show which account is connected and to post correctly.
  • Settings, the posting schedule, small video thumbnails and an upload log.

3. Where data is stored

All of the above is stored only on your computer, in the App's data folder (%APPDATA%\FlexVideoUploader). Videos are read from where you keep them and are not copied.

4. Where data is sent

Your videos and captions are sent only to TikTok and/or Facebook, through their official APIs, and only to the accounts you connected, when a post is due or when you click "Post now". Their handling of that data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Deleting your data

  • Uninstall the App and delete the %APPDATA%\FlexVideoUploader folder to remove everything it stored.
  • Revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings › Security › Apps and services) and in Facebook (Settings › Business integrations).

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
